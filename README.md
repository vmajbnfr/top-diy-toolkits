### **FAQ**

**How do I choose the right tool brand for my needs?**
For occasional DIY tasks, budget-friendly brands offer great value. For professional or heavy daily use, investing in high-end, durable brands is often more cost-effective in the long run.

**Is it better to buy tools online or in-store?**
Online stores often provide a wider selection and competitive pricing, while physical stores allow you to handle the tools before buying and offer immediate availability. Many of the best platforms combine both.

**What's the best way to start building a tool collection?**
Begin with a quality basic hand tool kit (hammer, screwdrivers, pliers, tape measure) and then buy specific power tools as your project needs dictate. A versatile cordless drill is often the first power tool people buy.
